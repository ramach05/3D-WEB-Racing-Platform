Used as the shared high-detail mesh for all garage body variants (hatchback / sedan / pickup). Sedan and pickup apply a gentle length-preserving whole-mesh warp (axles move, mild stance/roof change) plus light overlays or bed rails; hatchback is the authored mesh unchanged — not separate GLB files.
